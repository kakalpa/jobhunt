# Finnish Job Market Tailor

## When to Use This Skill

Activate this skill when:
- Applying for jobs located in Finland (Helsinki, Turku, Tampere, Oulu, Kuopio, Espoo, or remote in Finland)
- Applying to Finnish public-sector or municipal in-house companies (e.g., Istekki, Kela, HUS, wellbeing services counties / *hyvinvointialueet*, Kuntarekry, Valtiolle.fi)
- The applicant is an international professional based in Finland
- Preparing applications that involve Finnish language requirements, collective agreements (TES / KT-sopimus), or security clearances (*Supo perusmuotoinen turvallisuusselvitys*)

---

## Finnish Recruitment Cultural Rules & Best Practices

### 1. Factual Precision Over Exaggeration
* Finnish recruiters and engineering leads strongly dislike American-style hyperbole ("world-class visionary", "rockstar guru").
* **Do:** Be understated, direct, and factual. Let verifiable facts, metrics (e.g., "reduced tickets by 60%"), and completed projects speak for themselves.
* **Do:** State academic credentials clearly (e.g., *Insinööri (AMK)* / Bachelor of Engineering from a recognized Finnish institution like TUAS, GPA 4.0).

### 2. Language Transparency
* If the posting is in Finnish or mentions Finnish as a plus, **never pretend to be fluent if you are not**.
* Clearly state your language profile:
  - `English: Fluent / Professional working proficiency`
  - `Finnish: Basic – actively developing / daily language studies`
* Highlight that you can understand technical Finnish documentation and communicate clearly with bilingual colleagues. Finnish employers respect honest self-assessment.

### 3. The Finnish "Secret Weapon": Calling the Contact Person
* Unlike in the US/UK where calling a hiring manager unannounced is considered intrusive, in Finland **it is customary and welcomed** when an ad lists a contact person with a phone number and calling hours (e.g., *"Lisätietoja antaa Jukka Hytönen, puh. 044..."*).
* A 3–5 minute polite phone call asking 2 thoughtful, specific technical questions guarantees your name is remembered when applications are screened.
* Always prepare a call script for the candidate.

### 4. Public Sector & Collective Agreements (*KT-sopimus / AVAINTA*)
* Municipal and wellbeing county companies (like Istekki) operate under public sector collective agreements (*KT-yritykset* / *Avaintyönantajat AVAINTA*).
* Benefits include:
  - 36.25 hours/week standard working hours
  - Generous annual holiday accrual (up to 38 weekdays)
* Background checks: Positions often require a standard security clearance from the Finnish Security and Intelligence Service (*Suojelupoliisi / Supo*) and pre-employment drug screening. Explicitly state readiness for these.

### 5. Work Certificates (*Työtodistukset*) & References (*Suosittelijat*)
* Finnish employers take references seriously. Having a local Finnish reference (e.g., a university lecturer or Finnish company manager like Mr. Tero Virtanen from TUAS) adds immense credibility.
* Have diplomas and transcripts from Finnish institutions readily available.

## Output Deliverables

When tailoring an application for the Finnish market:
1. **Recruiter Phone Call Script:** A 3-minute phone guide with 2 intelligent questions to ask the contact person.
2. **Bilingual Form Q&A:** Provide both English and clean Finnish versions for standard portal text boxes.
3. **ATS-Ready Clean PDF:** Standard A4 format, concise 2-page limit, crisp typography.
4. **Transparent Language Phrasing:** Clear, professional phrasing regarding Finnish language acquisition.
